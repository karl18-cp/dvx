# Trainee team assignments

Admins can open **Trainees → Assign team leader** on a trainee row. The modal lists teams in that trainee's training campaign with an active team leader. The row shows the assigned team and leader after saving. Trainees also appear in the existing Team Assigning workflow.

Team leaders can use **My Team → Assign team member** to select an unassigned agent or trainee, or move someone between teams they already lead. Trainee choices are limited to the selected team's training campaign. Leaders cannot claim a trainee from another leader's team or assign anyone to teams they do not lead. Admins handle transfers between leaders.

Assigned trainees are included in My Team, attendance viewing and corrections, Employee Status, and existing team-scoped assessment/coaching views. A trainee's coaching created before team assignment remains visible to the currently assigned leader even when the record has no team snapshot. Records associated with another team's history remain restricted. Coaching creation and editing retain their existing admin/QA permissions.

No account roles, training campaigns, schedules, training plans, attendance history or allowance records are changed by assignment. Active and Passed trainees can be assigned. Failed accounts cannot receive a new assignment. Assignments are audited. A leader may handle multiple teams through the existing team leader assignments.

Validation covers admin assignment, campaign matching, active-leader requirements, own-team access, attendance correction, coaching visibility, cross-team transfer and unauthorized attempts.
