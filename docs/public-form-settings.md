# Public form settings

Main administrators can open **Management → Form Settings** to customize the application form and business inquiry form separately.

- Edit the form title, introduction, submit button text, field labels, and placeholders.
- Change dropdown choices, one option per line.
- Hide optional built-in fields or make them required. Essential fields stay visible and required to preserve recruitment, contact, and meeting workflows.
- Add extra text, long-text, email, number, date, dropdown, or checkbox questions. Extra questions can be required, hidden, or removed.
- Preview the form before saving. Changes become public only after **Save settings**.

Public submissions are validated against the saved settings. Extra answers appear in the applicant or business inquiry review and notification email. Each submission stores its question labels and values, so later edits do not rewrite historical answers.

The settings routes require an active main administrator. Other employee roles, including QA administrators, cannot modify public forms.
